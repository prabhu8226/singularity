03 — The Problem

Orbit is priced like a luxury good. It shouldn't be.

Five structural failures keep space closed to most of the world — and India still has no reusable rocket of its own.

COST

$2,000–$6,000/kg to orbit

Prohibitively expensive. Most launch vehicles are only partially reusable, repeatedly driving up costs.

ACCESS

No access for developing nations

Long wait times and limited launch slots lock out startups, researchers, and emerging economies.

INDIA

India has no reusable rocket

No fully reusable Indian launch vehicle, no domestic liquid rocket engine. India depends on solid motors for launches.

PROPELLANT

Dirty propellants dominate

RP-1, kerosene, and solid fuels pollute the atmosphere. No green propulsion standard exists yet.

EQUITY

Space is closed to most people

Individuals, universities, and small enterprises have no affordable pathway to send payloads to orbit.
